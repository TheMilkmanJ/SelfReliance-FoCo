# Self-Reliance FoCo v2.5.0

Seven new home-screen categories, filled with real Larimer and Colorado listings.

New tiles: **Child Care**, **Immigrants & Refugees**, **Addiction & Recovery**, **Kids with Special Needs**, **Holiday & Toys**, **Dental Care**, and **Disaster & Recovery**.

53 new cards (208 listings total). Existing food, health, and crisis cards stay put. Santa Cops is not listed (that program dissolved); holiday gifts go through NoCo Santa Squad. Teaching Tree, Health District Family Dental, SummitStone detox, Foothills Gateway, CCAP, UPK, Red Cross, NOCO Alert, and the rest are current as of September 2026.

versionCode 9 — installs over any earlier FoCo build, including v2.4.0, once a signed APK is cut from this tree.
