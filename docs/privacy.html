Privacy Policy — Self-Reliance FoCo

Effective date: October 6, 2026
Developer: Cream Top Labs / Justin Whitman
Contact: GitHub issues
App package: org.foco.selfreliance

Summary

Self-Reliance FoCo is a free Larimer County help directory. It does not collect personal data, does not require an account, and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs. Choices you make in the app stay on your device.

What the app does

The app shows a local directory of community resources (food, housing, shelter, jobs, child care, and related help) for Fort Collins, Loveland, Estes Park, Berthoud, Wellington, and Larimer County. You can search listings, filter by town or need, tap phone numbers to call, and open maps or websites for a listing.

Data we do not collect

We do not:

  • Create or require user accounts or sign-in
  • Collect names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, or income information
  • Use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • Sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties
  • Upload your taps, searches, or preferences to our servers

The app ships its resource list with the install. Directory content is not a live feed of your private activity.

Data stored on your device

Preferences such as city/area filter, language, dark mode, and trash-day neighborhood are stored only on your phone (local storage). Uninstalling the app removes that data. We cannot see those choices.

Permissions and system features

Calling a listed phone number or opening directions uses your phone’s dialer or maps apps. Those apps are separate products with their own privacy policies. This app does not store Google’s map data; offline map tips point you to download maps inside Google Maps yourself.

Android permissions are kept minimal. The published Expo config lists no special dangerous permissions for core directory use.

Children

The app is a public help directory suitable for all ages. It does not target children for data collection, show ads, or include user-generated chat. Parents and guardians may use it like any other reference tool.

Third-party links

Listings may include websites, phone numbers, and map links run by other organizations. When you leave this app, those organizations’ policies apply.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update this page and note the new effective date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
