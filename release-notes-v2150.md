# Self-Reliance FoCo v2.15.0

Trash day and Open now are on the Resources home screen. Not a fifth tab, and not a separate app.

**Trash day:** Fort Collins city-contracted homes pick their usual Republic weekday (Mon–Fri). The app remembers it on the phone and bumps collection one day when New Year’s, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, or Christmas falls Monday–Friday. Saturday holidays (July 4, 2026) do not move weekdays. Carts out by 7am. Recycling is the same day. Yard trimmings April–November. Extra-small 35-gallon trash carts are every other week. Call Republic at 970-416-2012 or look up the address on their Fort Collins page. Loveland uses the city’s Recollect calendar and a recycling-week toggle. Estes Park, Berthoud, and Wellington get hauler numbers, not a city-wide map.

This app cannot read Republic’s address GIS. Your usual weekday is stored on the phone only.

**Open now:** Walk-up meals, showers, beds, indoor heat/AC, and crisis lines with clock hours. Filter by Eat / Sleep / Shower / Sit / Weather / Call, and by Open now / Later today / All hours. Same desks as the directory. Hours change — call if you can.
